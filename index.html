WELCOME TO OCG

Who are We!

Oro Construction Group (OCG) is a distinguished construction and real estate development company committed to delivering quality projects across Ethiopia and beyond.

Through innovation, reliability and efficiency, OCG continues to contribute to national development through large-scale infrastructure, water, building and real-estate projects.

LEADERSHIP MESSAGE

Message from the CEO

Welcome to Oro Construction Group. We are committed to contributing to Ethiopia's development through quality construction, innovative solutions and sustainable investment.

At OCG, we believe that successful development goes beyond constructing buildings and infrastructure. It requires professionalism, integrity, innovation and a strong commitment to the communities we serve.

Our dedicated team and subsidiary companies continue to work together to deliver reliable, value-driven solutions while embracing new technologies and sustainable approaches.

As we look toward the future, we remain focused on strengthening our capabilities, building lasting partnerships and creating meaningful opportunities for economic and social development.

Dr. Eng. Messay Daniel Tullu
