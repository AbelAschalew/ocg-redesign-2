OUR PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

What Happens Next?

01
FIRST STEP

Submit Your Inquiry

Share information about your organization, capabilities and the partnership opportunity you would like to explore with OCG.

02
INITIAL REVIEW

We Review Your Proposal

Our team reviews the information provided to understand the opportunity, assess its relevance and identify potential areas of alignment.

03
DISCUSSION

Initial Conversation

Where appropriate, we may contact you to discuss your proposal, clarify requirements and explore how the organizations could work together.

04

Partnership Assessment

The proposed collaboration is evaluated based on strategic alignment, capabilities, project requirements and potential mutual value.

If there is a suitable opportunity to proceed, the parties can discuss the appropriate collaboration structure, responsibilities and next steps.
