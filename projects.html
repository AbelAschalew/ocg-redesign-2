03
STAGE 03

Procurement & Mobilization

We coordinate resources, materials, equipment and project teams to prepare the project for execution.

04
STAGE 04

Construction & Execution

Project teams execute the works while maintaining quality, safety, schedule and technical requirements.

05
STAGE 05

Progress, quality, safety, resources and performance are continuously monitored throughout project execution.

Upon completion, we conduct final inspections, address outstanding requirements and prepare the project for handover.
