Who are Our Subsidiaries?

Oro-Construction Group (OCG) encompasses five distinguished subsidiaries, each contributing uniquly to the broader mission of the organization. Among these, Oromia Construction Corporation (OCC), OBM Construction Share Company, and Walabu Construction Share Company are recognized as Grade I general contractors, while the Engineering Corporation of Oromia (ECO) holds the distinction of being Grade I consultancy services provider. IJA Developers Share Company, the newest addition to OCG, is dedicated to real estate, hotel, and tourism development. Together, these subsidiaries form a robust network that enables OCG to deliver comprehensive and integrated solutions across the construction and infrastructure sectors in Ethiopia. Collectively, our subsidiaies form a formidable team committed to excellence, sustainability, and community development, driving the growth and transformation of Ethiopia's built environment.

IJA Developer Share Company

Real estate development, hospitality, and tourism ventures.

Established in 2021, IJA Developers represents a new generation of property development, with an ambitious vision to become a leading and reliable developer in the hotel and tourism sectors in Africa. Its business portfolio comprises the development of tourism destinations, parks and recreational centers, convention centers, and real estate ventures, alongside tour and tourism services. With a growing workforce of 331, IJA is already managing some of Ethiopia’s premier destinations, such as Wanchi Dendi Ecotourism and the Langano lakeside resorts, demonstrating its commitment to enhancing the tourism experience in the region.
Responsible for Hotel, Tourism Destinations and Real Estate Development.

Oromia Construction Corporation | OCC

Infrastructure, buildings, and water projects.

ECO, a state-owned consulting company, has been delivering study, design, and supervision services to both local and international clients since 2006. Its comprehensive offerings include the design and construction supervision of buildings, roads, bridges, dams, water supply systems, irrigation projects, and hydropower initiatives. ECO boasts significant experience in integrated land use planning, urban planning and design, Environmental and Social Impact Assessments, and the planning of special economic zones, ensuring that projects are not only functional but also environmentally responsible and community-oriented.
Responsible for wide range of large-scale construction and infrastructure projects in Ethiopia, including water works (supply, dams, irrigation, drilling), transport infrastructure (roads, bridges, railways, airfields), and building construction (hospitals, schools, government offices, commercial/residential buildings).

Walabu Construction Share Company

Study, design, supervision, and engineering consulting

Walabu Construction Share Company is one of Ethiopia’s leading construction firms, recognized primarily for its expertise in constructing industrial buildings. With a substantial contract portfolio totaling around 86 million USD, Walabu has played a key role in developing industrial parks across various regions of the country. The company has achieved an annual turnover of approximately 24 million USD, reflecting its strong position in the market and commitment to delivering high-quality projects.
Responsible for road construction, building projects (like agro-industrial parks), and general infrastructure development in Ethiopia, working on government contracts and aiming to solve construction challenges through quality work, says its CEO. They handle residential, commercial, road works (URRAP, community roads), and infrastructure like irrigation and dams, often in partnership with state bodies like Oromia Industrial Parks.
