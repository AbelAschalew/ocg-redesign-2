About OCG

Building Ethiopia's Future Through Quality, Innovation and Excellence.

Company Profile

Oro Construction Group (OCG) is a distinguished construction and real estate development company based in Ethiopia. OCG undertakes large and complex construction and infrastructure projects

OCG fosters innovation by embracing emerging technologies and modern building methods. The group had a positive impact on its clients, employees, and community through the stability and resources built over years, including

  • Five subsidiary companies
  • More than 5200 employees
  • Asset valued at 175 Million USD
  • Annual turnover is 138 Million USD
  • 500 projects each year

Our History

Oro-Construction Group (OCG) was officially established on September 21, 2021, under Oromia Regional Government Regulation No. 234/2022. This move was part of the Oromia regional state government's broader efforts, initiated around 2006, to bolster its economy and improve livelihoods by establishing and restructuring public enterprises.

OCG rapidly positioned itself as a premier construction and real estate development entity, starting with an authorized capital of Birr 15 billion and a paid-up capital of Birr 6.8 billion. It was formed to bring together key construction and engineering companies in the region, creating a unified force to undertake significant infrastructure and building projects.
