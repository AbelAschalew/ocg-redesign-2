About OCG

Building Ethiopia's Future Through Quality, Innovation and Excellence.

Company Profile

Oro Construction Group (OCG) is a distinguished construction and real estate development company based in Ethiopia. OCG undertakes large and complex construction and infrastructure projects

OCG fosters innovation by embracing emerging technologies and modern building methods. The group had a positive impact on its clients, employees, and community through the stability and resources built over years, including

  • Five subsidiary companies
  • More than 5200 employees
  • Asset valued at 175 Million USD
  • Annual turnover is 138 Million USD
  • 500 projects each year
